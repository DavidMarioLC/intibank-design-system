## Brand & Style

The design system blends contemporary fintech precision with the warmth of Andean heritage. Inspired by _Inti_ (the sun deity) and traditional terracotta tones, the system avoids folklore pastiche, expressing cultural identity through chromatic balance, structured light, and grounding deep indigo tones.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor

- **Solar & Reassuring:** Financial actions evoke optimism, clarity, and empowerment without visual noise.
- **Rooted Modernity:** Warm stone surfaces replace sterile clinical grays; terracotta brings tactile earthiness, anchored by nighttime indigo for institutional credibility.
- **Architectural Flat:** Visual language leans on crisp surface containment, tonal separation, and refined hairline borders rather than gratuitous drop shadows or skeuomorphism.

### Target Audience

Peruvian digital-native consumers, young entrepreneurs, and mobile-first retail banking users who expect instant, frictionless financial transactions backed by an empathetic and culturally resonant presence.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around solar energy anchored by institutional depth and warm Andean earth.

### Core Swatches & Roles

- **Solar Amber (`#F59E0B` / Hover: `#D97706`):** Primary action color, focal states, and core brand identifiers. High visibility against both warm stone and indigo surfaces.
- **Andean Terracotta (`#DE5C22` / Hover: `#C24A16`):** Secondary interactive tier, promotional accents, card tags, and warm indicators.
- **Deep Nightfall Indigo (`#1E1B4B`):** Structural anchor used for primary banking cards, navigation rails, high-tier hero containers, and high-contrast dark surfaces.
- **Andean Warm Neutrals:**
  - Base canvas: `#FAFAF9` (warm stone)
  - Surface cards & containers: `#F5F5F4`
  - Elevated/overlay cards: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle architectural borders: `#E7E5E4`
  - Deep carbon text: `#2E2A26`
  - Muted/secondary text: `#78716C`
- **Semantic Feedback:**
  - Success: `#16A34A`
  - Warning / Pending: `#EAB308`
  - Critical / Destructive: `#DC2626`

### Usage Rules

- Keep primary Amber `#F59E0B` coupled with `#1E1B4B` or `#2E2A26` text to satisfy WCAG AA contrast standards.
- Reserve `#1E1B4B` for anchor moments (e.g., account balance summaries, debit card shells, primary header modules) to maintain institutional weight.
- Avoid multi-color gradients. Color transitions must rely on solid block layering and fine structural strokes.

## Typography

**Hanken Grotesk** serves across display, body, and label roles. Its geometric balance provides the systematic clarity expected of digital banking, while humanist terminal cuts echo craft and approachability.

### Typographic Hierarchy

- **Currency & Balances:** `numeric-currency` enforces tabular figures where possible to guarantee zero jitter across dynamic live updates and transactions.
- **Headlines:** Dense line height and subtle negative letter-spacing project fiscal confidence and clarity.
- **Data & Microcopy:** `body-sm` and `label-sm` maintain legibility on dense transaction ledgers and mobile receipt views.

## Layout & Spacing

The system applies a disciplined **8pt baseline spatial grid** to align monetary values, account modules, and interaction points.

### Screen Adaptations

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout, `1rem` screen margin, minimum touch target of 48px height. Modals open as bottom sheets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column fluid layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Primary card stack switches to a 2-column balance layout.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of 1280px with centered alignment, `2.5rem` outer margins, and `1.5rem` internal gutters.

### Content Reflow Rules

Transaction listings and account summary widgets transition from edge-to-edge full-bleed cards on small screens to structural contained cards on tablet and desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an authentic, clutter-free flat aesthetic, the design system dismisses heavy blurred shadows in favor of **structural layering, tone shifts, and hairline borders**.

### Layering Philosophy

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FAFAF9` warm stone canvas.
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers):** `#F5F5F4` for grouped panels, statement lists, and non-actionable cards.
- **Level 2 (Active Elements & Elevated Cards):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px solid #E7E5E4` border. No drop shadows in default resting state.
- **Level 3 (Floating Sheets, Popovers & Dialogs):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(46, 42, 38, 0.08)` and a bounding hairline border of `#E7E5E4`.
- **Anchor Panels:** `#1E1B4B` indigo blocks act as a stark tonal baseline for key metrics and physical card representations, relying entirely on surface contrast rather than elevation tokens.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level **2** (`0.5rem` base), extending gracefully to `1rem` (rounded-lg) and `1.5rem` (rounded-2xl) to project modern digital agility while remaining grounded.

### Shape Tokens in Context

- **Inputs & Action Chips:** `0.5rem` (rounded-md) to `0.75rem` for compact precision.
- **Cards & Summary Panels:** `1rem` (rounded-xl) for standard transaction cards; `1.5rem` (rounded-2xl) for hero balance modules and indigo card envelopes.
- **Badges & Notification Pills:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata from structural content.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary:** Filled in Solar Amber `#F59E0B` with `#1E1B4B` text, 48px height, `rounded-xl`, bold label. Hover: `#D97706`. Focus rings: 2px offset with `#F59E0B`.
- **Secondary / Terracotta:** Filled in `#DE5C22` with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for time-sensitive or promotional banking CTAs.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent surface with hairline `#E7E5E4` border, `#2E2A26` text, shifting to `#F5F5F4` background on hover.

### Chips & Filters

- Compact `rounded-full` height (32px), `1px solid #E7E5E4`, warm stone `#FAFAF9` background.
- Selected state: Indigo `#1E1B4B` background with white text, without border expansion.

### Form Inputs & Monetary Fields

- Resting: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E5E4`, text `#2E2A26`, radius `0.75rem`.
- Active/Focus: Border `2px solid #F59E0B`, zero intrusive blur glow.
- Currency field prefixes (e.g., _S/._) styled in secondary `#78716C` at `headline-md` scale.

### Avatars

- Initials avatars use a rounded-square silhouette with a `25%` radius, warm
  `#F5ECE6` surface, `1px solid #E7E5E4` border, and dark brown `#744300`
  initials in semibold Hanken Grotesk.
- Sizes follow the component grid: 40px (`sm`), 56px (`md`), and 72px (`lg`),
  with proportional 14px, 20px, and 28px initials. Medium is the default.
- Content is limited to one or two consumer-provided initials; the component
  does not derive, truncate, or transform names.
- The approved screenshot is a scaled presentation reference. Runtime sizes are
  normalized to the system grid while preserving its hairline border, centered
  type, warm palette, and rounded-square proportions.

### Badges

- Status badges use a 40px fully rounded pill with 24px inline padding, a 12px
  gap, a 12px circular indicator, and 20px/24px semibold Hanken Grotesk text.
- Semantic treatments pair pale surfaces with readable text and a brighter
  indicator: success uses `#EAF6ED` / `#0F7A37` / `#16A34A`; warning uses
  `#FFF7E6` / `#9A5D00` / `#F5B400` with a `#F8DCA3` border; danger uses
  `#FEE2E2` / `#B91C1C` / `#DC2626` with a `#FCA5A5` border.
- Labels must be concise, visible, and meaningful without color. The circular
  indicator is decorative and does not replace status text.
- The approved screenshots use different presentation scales. Runtime geometry
  is normalized to one system size, and foregrounds are darker than the bright
  accents where needed to preserve readable contrast.

### Dynamic Tokens

- Dynamic token cards use a white surface, neutral 1px border, 24px radius,
  48px desktop padding, and no resting shadow. The hierarchy is an indigo lock
  and 32px/40px heading with status Badge, muted 20px/28px guidance, grouped
  code, then expiry metadata and resend action below a divider.
- The six-digit code is grouped visually as `3–3`. Desktop digit cells target
  88×104px with a warm `#FFF8F4` surface, neutral border, 24px radius, and
  48px/56px semibold indigo numerals.
- Below 640px, spacing and type compact, digit cells scale down to 40×56px, and
  header or footer content may stack without changing digit order. The resend
  control retains a 48px minimum target.
- The code remains one ordered, digit-spaced value for assistive technology;
  visual cells, separator, lock, and timer icons are decorative. Timer updates
  are not announced every second, and status meaning is always present in text.
- The component only presents consumer-controlled code, time, status, and copy.
  It does not generate, store, rotate, validate, or transmit authentication
  data, and it does not schedule its own countdown.

### OTP Inputs

- OTP entry uses six ordered cells with a white surface, neutral 1px border,
  12px radius, 64px desktop height, and 32px/40px semibold indigo digits.
  Filled cells use the warm `#FFF8F4` surface. Focus retains the institutional
  amber `#F59E0B` inset boundary and adds a 2px deep-indigo `#1E1B4B` outer
  outline that exceeds the 3:1 non-text contrast target without layout shift.
- The six cells are one logical native input rather than six independent form
  fields. This preserves native selection, caret keys, deletion, full-code
  paste, form submission, numeric keyboards, one-time-code autofill, and one
  accessible label and value. The mirrored cells are decorative.
- Native range selection is mirrored with white digits on a deep-indigo
  surface so selecting part or all of the code remains visually apparent even
  though the underlying input text and platform selection are transparent.
- In containers narrower than 384px, gaps and cells contract to a 48px minimum
  height without changing digit order or introducing horizontal overflow.
  Invalid state uses `#DC2626`; disabled and read-only states use warm neutral
  surfaces and retain visible cell boundaries.
- OtpInput accepts only a controlled string of up to six ASCII digits. It does
  not generate, send, verify, expire, persist, transmit, or automatically
  submit a code; the surrounding authentication flow owns those operations.
- DynamicToken remains the read-only display for a code generated by the
  system. OtpInput is the editable control used to enter a code obtained from a
  separate channel or device.

### Cards & Ledger Lists

- **Balance Hero Card:** Deep Night Indigo `#1E1B4B` background, white label copy, primary Solar Amber `#F59E0B` metrics, subtle hairline border in `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`.
- **Transaction Item:** Clean flat list format on `#FFFFFF` with hairline divider `#E7E5E4`. Icon container rendered in rounded squares (`0.75rem`) tinted with semantic backgrounds (e.g., `#16A34A` 10% alpha).

### Checkboxes & Radios

- Size: 20x20px with a 4px inner border radius on checkboxes and circular for radios.
- Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` with `1.5px solid #E7E5E4`.
- Checked: Solar Amber `#F59E0B` fill with `#1E1B4B` icon mark.
